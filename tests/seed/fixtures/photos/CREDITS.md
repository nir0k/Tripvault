# Photographs of the stand's examples

The pictures the test stand's trips and ideas are seeded with come from
Wikimedia Commons, under the free licences named below. Each was reduced to
1024 pixels and recompressed for the fixtures; the pictures of the Iceland
report were given the date and position of the day they illustrate, so the
galleries read in the order of the trip. They are examples of a test stand and
not part of the product.
